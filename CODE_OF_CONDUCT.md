# Code of Conduct

CodexLattice aims to be a technically rigorous and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas, code, benchmarks, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- give specific, actionable review feedback;
- distinguish measured results from hypotheses or preferences;
- be respectful across languages, backgrounds, experience levels, and time zones;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they materially affect a technical recommendation;
- respect privacy and never pressure contributors to share private prompts, credentials, account data, or proprietary code.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, and bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is welcome when it improves the project. Prefer reproducible examples, tests, traces, and benchmark evidence. Maintainers may close discussions that repeatedly restate claims without new evidence or that become personal.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer when possible. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting private contact without including sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces controlled by CodexLattice maintainers.
